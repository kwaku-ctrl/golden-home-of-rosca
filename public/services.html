SUSU Savings

Secure group savings

Structured savings cycles for church groups, friends, and family circles.

Features

  • Automated savings schedules
  • Group payout tracking
  • Mobile-friendly access

Benefits

  • Costs spread across members
  • Group accountability
  • Higher saving discipline

Requirements

  • Group leader registration
  • Valid ID and phone number
  • Minimum member count of 5
How long does a SUSU cycle last?

Cycles can be flexible, typically between 4 and 13 weeks depending on group decision.
