Our story

Golden Home Of ROSCA began as a community vision for responsible savings and access to credit. We combine Ghanaian ROSCA traditions with digital convenience so more families can grow their wealth together.

Mission & vision

Our mission is to empower micro-entrepreneurs, church groups, and side hustlers with dependable savings and loan solutions. We envision a future where every savings circle has access to premium financial tools.
