FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about our savings, loans, and community banking services.

What is ROSCA and how does it work?

ROSCA is a rotating savings and credit association where group members contribute regularly and take turns receiving the pooled funds.

How do I join a SUSU savings circle?

Register with Golden Home Of ROSCA, create or join a group, and start contributing according to the agreed cycle.

What documents do I need for a loan application?

We normally require your Ghana Card, proof of address, and supporting income or business evidence.

Can I apply for both personal and business loans?

Yes. You can choose the product that fits your goal, and our eligibility checker will guide you.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use secure authentication and encryption practices to keep user data protected.
